Parent Portal

Set up The Hero Shift on your kid's device, then link your own phone to pause play, add time, send messages and say yes or no to requests.

Which device is this?

For grown-ups

No accounts and no sign-up. Your kid's settings live only on their device, and only a nickname is used. When you link your phone, the two devices talk directly to each other. A free pairing server (PeerJS) only helps the two devices find each other, and nothing is stored on it. To cut a phone off, choose “Unlink all phones” on the kid's device and a new code is made.
