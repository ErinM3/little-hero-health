Find your hero group

Little Hero Health is for heroes aged 5 to 25. Each group plays a different way, so little kids get pretend play and teens and grown-ups get real hospital work.

How old are you?

🧸
Ages 5 to 12

Little Heroes

Pretend hospital with teddy bear patients. Kids learn that doctors and hospitals are safe and fun.

  • Pretend check-ups: heartbeat, temperature, bandages
  • Picture patient cards
  • Hero badges and stickers
  • The Hero Box kit at home

No chat. A grown-up books every event.

Play in the Workstation
🩺
Ages 13 to 17

Teen Heroes

Real hospital shifts. Triage patients, read vital signs, hand off to the next shift and explore health careers.

  • Triage with the 5 levels real ERs use
  • Made-up patients with vitals and history
  • SBAR handoffs, like real nurses
  • Ranks from Volunteer to Attending

Practice only, never medical advice. Teens chat only with teens.

Ages 18 to 20

Young Pros

Lead and teach. Run teams on shifts, help at camps and write practice cases for teens.

  • Team lead on Teen Heroes shifts
  • Camp counselor and event staff
  • Harder cases with surprises
  • Links to real CNA and EMT courses

Event staff need a background check and safety training.

Ages 21 to 25

Pro Heroes

Run Little Hero Health with Harry: lead events, mentor younger heroes and work with hospital partners.

  • Grown-up Admin and event lead
  • Mentor teens in group settings
  • Invite real nurses and EMTs to speak
  • Check new cases for safety

Every in-person event has a Pro Hero or parent in charge.

Go to Camp HQ

Safety for every group

  • Kids under 13 never chat, and a parent or guardian books their tickets.
  • Teens and adults are kept in separate chat groups. No private messages between adults and kids.
  • All patients in the game are made up. Little Hero Health never gives real medical advice.
  • If anything feels wrong, stop and tell a grown-up.
