Find your hero group

The Hero Shift is for heroes aged 5 to 18. Each group plays a different way: little kids get pretend play, and older kids and teens get real hospital thinking.

How old are you?

🧸
Ages 5 to 11

Little Heroes

Pretend hospital with teddy bear patients. Kids learn that doctors and hospitals are safe and fun.

  • Pretend check-ups: heartbeat, temperature, bandages
  • Talk to Biscuit the Bear about how he feels
  • Picture puzzles in the Puzzle Lab
  • Hero badges, stickers and the Hero Box at home

No chat. A grown-up books every event.

Talk to Biscuit

Ages 12 to 15

Junior Heroes

Real hospital thinking. Spot vital signs that are off, decide who goes first and solve mystery patients.

  • Vital signs detective
  • Who goes first? Three-level triage
  • First aid steps in the right order
  • Mystery patients with clues

Practice only, never medical advice. Chat is for teens only, with a parent's OK.

Ages 16 to 18

Senior Heroes

Real hospital shifts. Triage with the five levels real ERs use, hand off with SBAR and practice medicine math.

  • Five-level ER triage
  • SBAR handoffs, like real nurses
  • Medicine math practice
  • Helper roles at events, with a grown-up in charge

Practice only. Real care is always given by licensed staff.

Safety for every group

  • Kids under 13 never chat, and a parent or guardian books their tickets.
  • Players are 18 and under. Grown-ups join only as parents, guardians or event staff, and never send private messages to kids.
  • All patients in the game are made up. The Hero Shift never gives real medical advice.
  • If anything feels wrong, stop and tell a grown-up.
