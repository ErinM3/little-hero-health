Puzzle Lab

Hospital problem-solving games for every age group. Pick your group, choose a puzzle and play a short round. Every answer comes with the reason why, and there are no timers.

For grown-ups

The Puzzle Lab teaches how hospital teams think: sorting who needs help first, reading vital signs and following safe steps. It is a learning game, not medical training or advice. In a real emergency, call 911. Medicine math uses a made-up practice medicine only. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere: stars are saved only on this device.
