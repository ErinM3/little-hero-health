Community rules

  • Be kind. Every hero is welcome.
  • Use your hero name. Never share your real name, school, address, phone number or photos of your face.
  • Don't accept friend requests or DMs from people you don't know. Tell a moderator if anyone makes you uncomfortable.
  • No bullying, swearing, spam or selling. Moderators can remove anyone who breaks the rules.
  • Follow Discord's own rules and terms.

Safety on Discord

  • Since 2026, Discord treats every account as a teen until the person verifies they're an adult, with stricter message filters and sensitive images blurred. These protections can't be turned off by server owners.
  • Families can see who their teen talks to with Discord's Family Center.
  • Our server is run by The Hero Shift moderators, with a grown-up on the moderation team. Voice rooms are only open when a moderator is there.
For heroes under 13

Discord is for ages 13 and up

You can still cheer on your friends:

  • Play a shift together in real life with the The Hero Shift game.
  • Send kind, ready-made high fives to your camp friends in Camp HQ.
  • Ask a grown-up to come back with you when you turn 13.
