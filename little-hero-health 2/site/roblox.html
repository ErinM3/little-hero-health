Roblox · Maple Hospital

Play Little Hero on Roblox

After you play a shift in real life, join your friends for a group shift on our Little Hero server inside Maple Hospital, the biggest hospital game on Roblox.

Maple Hospital is made by Marizma Games. Our Little Hero servers are private servers we run inside it. We're not part of Marizma Games or Roblox.
How to join

Join with an invite

  1. Ask a grown-upOur servers are invite-only. Your family, camp leader or a Little Hero administrator shares the invite.
  2. Use your family's accountFamilies set up and look after their child's Roblox account and its safety settings.
  3. Pick your hero jobPlay the same job you played in real life: doctor, nurse, paramedic and more.
  4. Hero breakPlay 30 to 45 minutes, then stretch, drink water and tell someone your best moment.

Server links are never posted publicly. Only the app owner and app administrators create servers and share invites.

Server rules

Our Hero Code of conduct

  • Be kind. Every player is a hero and every patient gets care.
  • Use your hero name. Never share your real name, school, address or age.
  • If anything feels wrong, stop and tell a grown-up straight away.
  • Take turns with the best jobs, just like at camp.
For families and camps

Safety on Roblox

  • Roblox now requires an age check to use chat, worldwide since January 2026. Players who skip it can't chat.
  • Checked players chat with their own age group and the groups just above and below. For children under 9, chat is off unless a parent consents after an age check.
  • Camps: run Roblox sessions for heroes 13 and up. Ages 9 to 12 need family consent and a staff member at every screen. Under 9s play the Little Hero game instead.
  • Families create and own their child's account. Camps never create kids' accounts or keep Roblox logins.
  • Keep chat off for camp sessions through Roblox parental controls; heroes talk to each other in the room.
